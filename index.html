Purpose Score Methodology

Our internal Purpose Score (0–100) audits every item across four weighted pillars:

1. Material Longevity (35%) — Resistance to corrosion and daily wear.
2. Repairability & Spare Parts (30%) — Availability of replaceable seals and parts.
3. Recyclability (20%) — Mono-material construction or home compostability.
4. Packaging Restraint (15%) — Zero single-use plastic mailers or foam.
